About Us

Our Story

Mountain Tree Service is a fully licensed, bonded, and insured local tree company serving Vacaville and surrounding Solano County communities. Started in January 2018, the company was built around straightforward communication, careful tree work, and clean results for homeowners, yards, rentals, and properties.

Led by Anastasia, Matt, and Tim, Mountain Tree Service handles pruning, removals, storm cleanup, stump work, cabling, planting, and yard clearing with the kind of direct service people expect from a family-owned local business.
