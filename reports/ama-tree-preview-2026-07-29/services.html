Services

Complete tree care for homes, yards, and problem trees.

AMA Tree handles structural support, tree health concerns, planting, pruning, stump removal, transplanting, and removals for Vallejo-area properties.

Tree cabling or bracing

Support for weak unions, heavy limbs, and trees that need structural help before damage gets worse.

Disease management - trees

Visual tree-health checks and practical recommendations when foliage, bark, dieback, or growth patterns look wrong.

Insect management - trees

Help identifying pest pressure and planning next steps for stressed or visibly damaged trees.

Tree planting

Planting help for shade, privacy, curb appeal, and better long-term placement around homes and fences.

Tree pruning or trimming

Clearance, shaping, roof-line pruning, canopy reduction, and maintenance for safer yards and cleaner views.

Tree stump removal

Remove or grind remaining stumps to reduce trip hazards and make the space usable again.

Tree transplanting

Relocation support when an existing tree needs to move because of access, construction, layout, or growth conflicts.

Tree removal

Removal help for dead, leaning, damaged, crowded, or poorly placed trees that need professional handling.

Clear estimates

  • Photos by text so the crew can understand scope before visiting
  • Cleanup, hauling, stump, planting, transplanting, disease, and insect concerns included in the request
