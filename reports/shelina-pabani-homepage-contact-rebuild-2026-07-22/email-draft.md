Subject: A stronger direction for your website

Hi Shelina,

Thanks for reaching out. I took a look at your current site and did some quick homework on your public footprint. The strongest thing I noticed is that you already have the credibility that most consultants would struggle to build: enterprise experience, strong client proof, meaningful testimonials, and a clear specialty around leadership and employee development.

Where I think we can help is making the site feel more aligned with the level of work you actually do.

Right now, the site explains who you are and what you offer, but it reads more like an older brochure site than a premium leadership consulting presence. For a buyer considering leadership training, manager development, or an executive/team offsite, the site should quickly make it obvious:

- what problems you help solve
- which service path fits them
- why you are credible
- what to send you to start the conversation

The main changes are:

- stronger executive positioning
- more immediate visibility for your enterprise credibility and representative client experience
- a cleaner brand/header direction so the logo area feels integrated instead of pasted on
- a more restrained portrait treatment, with the headshot supporting the career story instead of dominating the page
- stronger use of public credibility signals, including your Apple, Walmart.com, Gap Inc., and enterprise learning background
- a more intentional client-proof section with color and structure
- service framing around real business moments, not just a list of training topics
- stronger use of testimonials as proof that your facilitation works in the room
- a contact section built around qualified consulting requests instead of a generic form

My recommendation would be to start with a focused homepage and contact rebuild first, rather than turning this into a large full-site project right away. That would directly address your goal of getting more requests while making the site feel more appropriate for the type of corporate clients and leadership work you support.

For any client logos or public images, we would confirm what you want shown before launch. The concept uses them to show the kind of credibility that should be much easier for a serious buyer to see.
