# Shelina Pabani Consulting - Executive Homepage / Contact Rebuild Notes

Goal from inquiry: **More requests**

## What the current site already has

- Strong credibility and relevant experience.
- Clear service domain: leadership and employee development consulting.
- Useful proof: testimonials, clients, partnerships, and facilitator feedback.
- Enterprise-level credibility, including Apple, Walmart.com, Prosper, VMware, Google, Salesforce, Equinix, Facebook, Intercom, Shutterfly, Method, and related client/partner proof shown on the current site.
- Existing Squarespace site structure that can be reused or migrated.
- A recognizable existing logo that should be retained and modernized rather than discarded. If that logo appears on business cards, proposals, or training materials, continuity matters.

## Main conversion issue

The current site reads like a credibility brochure. It explains Shelina's background and offerings, but it does not immediately communicate the level of client, buyer, and business problem she is equipped to support.

For this kind of consultant, the site should not feel like a small local service site or a generic lead-generation landing page. It should feel appropriate for corporate learning leaders, HR executives, founders, and senior teams evaluating a high-trust leadership development partner.

For someone looking for leadership training, manager development, or a team offsite, the site should quickly answer:

- Is this for my type of organization?
- What kind of problem can Shelina help with?
- What is the next step?
- What information should I send to start the conversation?

## Changes shown in the mockup

1. More executive positioning

   The new hero moves away from a generic consulting description and positions Shelina around higher-stakes leadership moments:

   **Leadership development for organizations where the stakes are real.**

   This better matches the type of client and fee level her work appears to support.

2. Enterprise credibility moved above the fold

   The revised homepage quickly surfaces representative client proof from the current site, including companies such as VMware, Google, Salesforce, Equinix, Facebook, Intercom, Prosper, Shutterfly, Method, and Bank of the West.

   This tells a serious buyer that Shelina has worked in the right rooms before.

3. Prior Apple, Gap, and Walmart.com experience separated from client logos

   Public profiles show Shelina held major learning roles before consulting, including:

   - Director of Global Retail Training at Apple, supporting skill development for 60,000+ employees globally.
   - Head of Learning at Walmart eCommerce.
   - 13 years at Gap Inc., including responsibility for the Core Leadership Development Program.

   The mockup uses this as career credibility, not as a current-client claim.

4. Logo direction rebuilt into the site system

   The concept no longer drops the existing logo image into the navigation as a pasted asset. It uses an integrated SP monogram treatment with the site's charcoal, copper, and warm neutral palette. In a final build, this could either become the refreshed primary mark or be replaced with a cleaned-up extraction of the existing letterform if Shelina wants stricter continuity.

5. Portrait image reduced and moved into credibility context

   The headshot is no longer treated as a full hero panel. It appears as a smaller bio image beside a career credibility blurb, which keeps Shelina visible without letting the portrait overwhelm the homepage.

6. Client proof made more intentional

   The representative client logos are placed in a cleaner proof band with their color preserved and a subtle accent system. The goal is to make the logos feel like part of the page architecture, not like grayscale files pasted into empty boxes.

7. Service framing changed from "training catalog" to business moments

   The current site lists many programs and topics. The mockup groups them around buyer situations:

   - New manager and leadership development
   - Team offsites and executive alignment
   - Custom learning strategy and programs
   - Executive groups facing change

   This lets a visitor self-identify faster.

8. Stronger proof placement

   Shelina already has good testimonials and participant feedback. The mockup brings this proof higher on the page so it supports the decision to inquire.

9. Better positioning of Shelina's background

   The existing Apple, Walmart.com, and Prosper experience is valuable. The mockup presents this as a reason to trust her with corporate learning needs, not just as biography.

10. Contact section rebuilt around qualified requests

   Instead of a generic contact form, the new contact flow asks about:

   - organization
   - type of support needed
   - audience/challenge/timeline/outcome

   This should help produce better requests and make the first follow-up conversation more useful.

## Recommended first phase

A focused homepage/contact rebuild is the best first step.

Included:

- Homepage rewrite and visual redesign.
- Logo cleanup / brand refresh that preserves continuity.
- Public image and proof audit.
- Client-logo/proof section using approved marks only.
- Updated service framing.
- Stronger inquiry calls to action.
- Rebuilt contact/request flow.
- Mobile cleanup.
- Basic SEO/share metadata cleanup.

This avoids overbuilding while directly addressing the stated goal: more requests.
