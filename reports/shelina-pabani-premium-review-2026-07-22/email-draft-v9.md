Subject: Website direction for Shelina Pabani Consulting

Hi Shelina,

Thanks for reaching out. I took a close look at your current site and your public professional footprint. The biggest opportunity I see is not that you need more content. You already have strong credibility. The opportunity is making that credibility easier for a serious buyer to understand quickly.

Your background signals a much higher level of work than the current site communicates at first glance: leadership and learning roles at Apple, Walmart.com, and Gap Inc.; experience with enterprise teams; and strong proof that your facilitation helps people engage with difficult material in a clearer, more productive way.

I put together a homepage direction to show how I would recommend repositioning the site:

[Attach annotated screenshot]

The main shift is to move the site away from feeling like a general training-services page and toward a more premium consulting presence. The page should help a buyer quickly understand:

- when to bring you in
- what kind of leadership moments you help with
- why your enterprise background matters
- what outcomes your work supports
- what information to send when they are ready to start a conversation

The concept keeps the site focused around buyer needs: manager development, team alignment, learning at scale, and leaders navigating change. It also brings your enterprise credibility and client proof forward earlier, so visitors do not have to piece it together themselves.

My recommendation would be to start with a focused homepage and contact-section rebuild first. That gives you a stronger front door for qualified inquiries without turning this into a large, slow website project. From there, we can expand supporting pages only where they help conversion or credibility.

Before anything went live, we would confirm final copy, logos/client references, testimonials, and any role descriptions you want shown publicly.
