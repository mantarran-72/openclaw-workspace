Who is Gastelum?

Local tree care with the equipment to handle serious work.

Gastelum Tree Service helps homeowners and property managers keep outdoor spaces safe, clean, and usable. The crew handles everyday pruning and cleanup, but the real strength is larger work: removals, high limbs, storm response, stump grinding, landscape repairs, and irrigation service.

Every estimate starts with the practical details that matter on site: access, hazards, nearby structures, hauling, and cleanup. That keeps the job clear before the crew arrives and gives customers a better sense of what they are paying for.
